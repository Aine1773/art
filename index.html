Security First

Your keys.
Your crypto.
Your control.

OneArt Desktop Wallet is built on the principle of absolute self-custody. No company, no server, no third party can ever access your funds.

Private Key Ownership
Your keys, your crypto. Private keys are generated and stored locally — never transmitted to any server.
Local Encrypted Storage
All wallet data is encrypted with AES-256 and stored locally. Your assets remain under your complete control.
Self-Custody Architecture
OneArt is a non-custodial wallet. No accounts, no sign-ups, no third-party custody of your funds.
Secure Desktop Architecture
Built with process isolation, memory encryption, and anti-phishing protection for desktop-grade security.
